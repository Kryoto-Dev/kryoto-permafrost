# Changelog

## [0.1.0] - unreleased

### Added

- **Freeze a game folder.** Pick a folder and get one small setup, or a setup with `.kryo` files beside it. Three modes: Frost, Deep Freeze and Absolute Zero.
- **Size and ratio, all the way through.** An estimate before you start, the ratio and projected size while it runs, and a breakdown of where the size went when it finishes.
- **Find on Kryoto.** Fills in a game's title, version, program and cover.
- **A setup of its own.** Your cover art frozen in ASCII ice that melts as the game installs, with its own music and the game's icon.
- **Optional parts.** Languages and bonus content go in their own files, so players can skip the download.
- **Checked twice.** Every byte is compared with the original after a freeze, and the setup checks the data again as it installs.
- **Stop and carry on.** A stopped freeze or install picks up from where it left off.
- **Uninstaller.** The game shows up in Windows' list of programs, and removing it leaves saves alone.
